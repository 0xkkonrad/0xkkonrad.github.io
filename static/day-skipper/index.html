When is your exam?

537 cards is more than you can cram. Give the app a date and it works out how many new cards a day you need, and stops scheduling anything for after you have sat it.

How this works

You are shown a question. Try to answer it in your head, then tap the card to see whether you were right, and say how it went.

Again means you did not know it. Hard means you got there, slowly. Good means you knew it. Easy means it was obvious. Be honest — the app uses your answer to decide when to show the card next, so flattering yourself just means seeing it less.

Cards you find hard come back within minutes. Cards you know come back in days, then weeks. That is the whole trick: you spend your time on the material you have not learned yet.

Nothing is uploaded. Your progress is stored in this browser, so back it up from the Progress tab before you change phone.

      Settings

      Install

      Offline

      The cards work offline as soon as you have opened the app once. The 24 diagrams are about 2 MB and are saved as you meet them — pull them all down now if you are heading somewhere without signal.

      Sync

      Study on your phone and your laptop and keep one set of progress. Turn it on here, then type the key it gives you into the other device. There is no account and no email: the key is the only way in, so keep it, and anyone you give it to shares your deck.

      Backup

      A backup is a file you keep, and it works whether or not sync is on. Export writes a small JSON file you can email to yourself; restore reads it back on any device.
